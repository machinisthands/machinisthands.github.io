Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use machinisthands.github.io and how it is used. It covers every part of the site: the front page, the Mechanical Workshop pages (including the pneumatics lab), the WorldSkills CNC Milling pages, and the About, Contact, Privacy and Disclaimer pages. The Machinist Hands blog on Blogger has its own privacy policy.

1. Information you give us

If you e-mail us, we receive your name, e-mail address and message. We use them only to reply to you. We do not sell them or share them with anyone.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages you request, to run and protect the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Advertising – Google AdSense and cookies

  • This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Because the site is used by students, every ad request asks Google for its treatment for users under 18, which limits ad personalisation.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the study material still works without them.

4. Views, likes and comments

Job pages, WorldSkills test project pages and the front page show views, like and dislike buttons, a Share button and comments.

  • View, like and dislike counts are stored as totals only in Google Firebase. Your own like or dislike is remembered in your browser (local storage) so the button shows your choice; it is not linked to your name.
  • To comment you sign in with your Google account. Your comment is stored in Google Firebase together with your Google name and profile picture, and they are shown publicly next to the comment. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time.
  • Google's Privacy Policy applies to Google sign-in and Firebase.

5. Other services and links

Pages link to YouTube videos, PDF files and other websites. When you open them, their own privacy policies apply; we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

6. Children

The site is made for college students and working machinists. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable care of the information we receive, but no transfer over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: machinisthands@gmail.com or the Contact page.
