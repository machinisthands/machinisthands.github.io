About us

About

Machinist Hands shares what people who work with machine tools have learned: how the work is done, what to study, and the experiences and opinions that come with it.

Who is a machinist?

A machinist is a skilled worker who operates, dismantles, reassembles and repairs machine tools, and makes new parts with them – gears, splines, shafts and much more – to the drawing and to the tolerance.

What you will find here

  • Mechanical Workshop jobs: the workshop jobs of TKM College of Engineering, grouped by year and semester. Each job has an interactive 3D model, the engineering drawing, a process guide and, where available, the CNC program, a machining video and questions.
  • WorldSkills CNC Milling: every WorldSkills CNC Milling test project from 2007 to 2019 in 3D, top and bottom, with the drawings to download.
  • Pneumatics lab: a 3D trainer where students connect the tubes of a pneumatic circuit themselves and learn from their own mistakes.
  • Machinist Hands blog (in Malayalam): what to study to become a machinist and how to study it, stories and memories from work and life, and opinions on education, skills and society.
  • Machinist Hands on YouTube: the same topics as videos, including machining demonstrations of the workshop jobs.

Why this site exists

Good study material for workshop practice is hard to find in one place, and much of it is only on paper. These pages put the drawing, the 3D part, the process and the program side by side, so a student can understand a job before standing at the machine – and come back to it afterwards. Everything here is free to use for learning.

A note on safety

The material here is for learning. It does not replace training at the machine or the instructions of your instructor. Always follow the workshop's safety rules and wear the right protective equipment. See the Disclaimer.
